Why the KEV catalog: the real-attack filter

  • KEV (Known Exploited Vulnerabilities) is the official catalog of CISA, the US cybersecurity agency: a CVE only gets in with confirmed exploitation.
  • It carries weight: US federal agencies have a mandatory deadline to fix whatever enters it (directive BOD 22-01).
  • Real, live data: public, free, and updated every week. It grew from 1,713 to 1,734 in 18 days.
